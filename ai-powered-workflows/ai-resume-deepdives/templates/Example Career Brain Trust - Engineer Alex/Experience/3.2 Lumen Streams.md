### 3.2 Lumen Streams, Software Engineer

**Dates:** March 2021 to June 2023
**Location:** Seattle, Washington
**Tags:** `#Engineering` `#Backend` `#Streaming` `#Infrastructure` `#Go` `#Python` `#Kafka` `#gRPC` `#ServiceMesh` `#SDK` `#Scale`
**Source priority:** LinkedIn profile (canonical, May 2026).

---

#### Canonical Bullets

- Built the stream ingestion service in Go consuming roughly 1.2 million events per minute from Apache Kafka topics across 64 partitions in production.
- Implemented exactly-once semantics for downstream consumers using checkpointing primitives, in collaboration with the storage team.
- Led the migration from Google Remote Procedure Call (gRPC) on bare metal to a containerized service mesh (Istio); cut 99th-percentile (p99) latency by ~38 percent and freed roughly 6 engineering-weeks of operational toil per quarter.
- Wrote the company's first Python Software Development Kit (SDK) for partner platforms; adopted by 4 partner integrations within the first year of general availability.
- Contributed to the engineering hiring loop; ran technical screens for ~30 candidates per quarter and helped redesign the screen to reduce false negatives.
- Maintained a small internal Go library for retry and backoff primitives; still in use across 8 backend services as of 2026.
