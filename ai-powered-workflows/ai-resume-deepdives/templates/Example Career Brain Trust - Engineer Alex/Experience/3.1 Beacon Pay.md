### 3.1 Beacon Pay, Senior Backend Engineer

**Dates:** July 2023 to Present
**Location:** Remote (Portland, Oregon)
**Tags:** `#Engineering` `#Backend` `#FinTech` `#Payments` `#Go` `#Distributed` `#Postgres` `#Citus` `#Idempotency` `#RFC` `#Mentor` `#Scale` `#Senior`
**Source priority:** LinkedIn profile (canonical, May 2026). Promoted to Senior Backend Engineer in April 2025.

---

#### Canonical Bullets

- Owned the transaction-processing service handling roughly 4 million transactions per day, written in Go and deployed across 4 Amazon Web Services (AWS) regions.
- Designed and shipped idempotency primitives for the payment Application Programming Interface (API), reducing duplicate-charge incidents by ~95 percent in the 12 months post-launch vs. the 12 months prior.
- Led the migration from single-instance PostgreSQL to a Citus-sharded cluster, hitting a 7 times throughput improvement with zero customer-facing downtime over a 4-month rollout.
- Built a payment routing service that dynamically selects between 5 acquirers based on cost and acceptance signals; the routing logic is now adopted by the cross-border team for currency routing.
- Wrote and shepherded 5 Request-for-Comments (RFCs) on backend architecture; 3 became core platform patterns adopted by adjacent teams.
- Mentored 2 mid-level engineers through their first major shipping projects; both have since led their own RFCs within 18 months.
- Run weekly office hours for newer engineers on the team; the office hours have grown from 2 attendees to a regular 8.
