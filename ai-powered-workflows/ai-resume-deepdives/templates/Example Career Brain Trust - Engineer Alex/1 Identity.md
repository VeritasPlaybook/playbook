# 1. Identity (Alex)

---

## Section 1A: LinkedIn Headline

> **Senior Backend Engineer | Go and Python | distributed payments infrastructure at scale | targeting Staff Individual Contributor (IC) roles**

Notes:

- Names the primary language (Go) first, then Python as the secondary signal.
- "Distributed payments infrastructure at scale" tells a Staff hiring manager what kind of complexity I've operated against.
- The explicit "targeting Staff IC roles" prevents recruiters from spamming Engineering Manager openings; I want to stay on the IC track.
- Last refresh: 2026-05-21.

---

## Section 1B: Professional Summary

> I'm a Senior Backend Engineer at Beacon Pay, working on the transaction-processing service in Go. I've spent roughly six years in backend infrastructure across payments, real-time streaming, and an early-stage Python startup. The work I'm proudest of: shipping idempotency primitives that cut duplicate-charge incidents by ~95 percent, and leading the migration from a single-instance Postgres to a sharded Citus cluster with zero customer-facing downtime.
>
> I write Request-for-Comments (RFCs) for new architecture, I mentor mid-level engineers, and I take on-call seriously. I'm looking for a Staff Individual Contributor role at a backend infrastructure, fintech, or developer-platform company where the technical depth is the work, not a side effect of the work.

## Section 1C: Positioning Library

### Distributed-systems framing

> "I work on systems where the wrong primitive in the wrong place becomes a multi-hour incident. Idempotency, exactly-once semantics, and sharded data flow are not abstract topics; they are the constraints I design around weekly at Beacon Pay."
**Tags:** `#Distributed` `#Backend` `#Senior`
**Use when:** the role explicitly calls for distributed systems experience or names primitives like idempotency, exactly-once, or sharded databases.

### Scale framing

> "The transaction-processing service I own at Beacon Pay handles roughly 4 million transactions per day across four Amazon Web Services (AWS) regions. The Postgres-to-Citus migration I led hit a 7 times throughput improvement with zero customer-facing downtime. Those are the constraints I want to keep working against."
**Tags:** `#Scale` `#Backend` `#Migration`
**Use when:** the role is at a scaled company or names throughput, latency, or zero-downtime migration as a signal.

### Builder framing

> "I built the first Representational State Transfer (REST) API at Hearthstone Labs from zero, through general availability, supporting the company's first 5,000 customers. I built the first Python Software Development Kit (SDK) at Lumen Streams. I'm comfortable starting from a blank repository."
**Tags:** `#Build` `#0to1`
**Use when:** the role is at an earlier-stage company or specifically names "from zero" or "founding engineer" language.

### Mentorship framing

> "I've mentored 2 mid-level engineers at Beacon Pay through their first major shipping projects; both went on to lead their own Request-for-Comments (RFCs) within 18 months. I ran the engineering hiring loop at Lumen Streams. I'm interested in Staff roles where mentorship and technical leadership are part of the work, not a sidecar to it."
**Tags:** `#Mentor` `#Staff` `#Leadership`
**Use when:** the role is Staff Engineer and the Job Description (JD) names mentorship, technical leadership, or hiring as part of the scope.

### On-call discipline framing

> "I take on-call seriously. The incident retrospectives I wrote at Beacon Pay are linked in my GitHub profile. The reason I write them is that the next person on-call should not have to discover what I already discovered."
**Tags:** `#OnCall` `#Reliability` `#Senior`
**Use when:** the role names on-call rotation as part of the responsibility and the team values incident discipline.

### Polyglot framing

> "Go is my primary; Python is my secondary. I'm comfortable reading and modifying Rust. The language is a means to the end; the end is the system that runs at 3:00 in the morning without anyone noticing."
**Tags:** `#Polyglot` `#Go` `#Python`
**Use when:** the role is language-agnostic but lists multiple languages and the JD wants someone who treats languages as tools rather than identities.

### Open-source framing

> "I contribute occasionally to a Go library for idempotency primitives that I help maintain. My GitHub is the lowest-friction way to see how I actually write code; the resume is a summary, the repository is the artifact."
**Tags:** `#OpenSource` `#Portfolio`
**Use when:** the role names open-source contribution as a plus or the JD links to the company's public repositories.
