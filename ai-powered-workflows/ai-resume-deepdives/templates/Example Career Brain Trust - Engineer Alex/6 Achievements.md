# 6. Achievements (Alex)

---

## Beacon Pay

- Reduced duplicate-charge incidents by ~95 percent through new idempotency primitives shipped to the payment Application Programming Interface (API).
  - *Source:* Internal incident log dashboard, snapshot March 2026 (12 months pre-launch vs. 12 months post-launch).
- Achieved a 7 times throughput improvement on the transaction-processing service through the Postgres-to-Citus migration, with zero customer-facing downtime.
  - *Source:* Internal load-test results, migration report, October 2024.
- Transaction-processing service handles roughly 4 million transactions per day across 4 Amazon Web Services (AWS) regions.
  - *Source:* Internal Datadog dashboard, snapshot April 2026.
- Shepherded 5 Request-for-Comments (RFCs) on backend architecture; 3 became core platform patterns adopted by adjacent teams.
  - *Source:* Internal RFC repository, March 2026.

---

## Lumen Streams

- Stream ingestion service handles roughly 1.2 million events per minute from Kafka topics across 64 partitions in production.
  - *Source:* Internal Datadog dashboard, snapshot May 2023.
- Cut 99th-percentile (p99) latency by ~38 percent through the gRPC service mesh migration; freed roughly 6 engineering-weeks of operational toil per quarter.
  - *Source:* Internal migration report, December 2022.
- Shipped the first Python Software Development Kit (SDK) for partner platforms; adopted by 4 partner integrations in the first year of general availability.
  - *Source:* Partnership integrations log, June 2023.

## Hearthstone Labs

- Built the core Representational State Transfer (REST) Application Programming Interface (API) in Python (FastAPI) supporting the company's first 5,000 customers from beta through general availability.
  - *Source:* Internal customer count snapshot, January 2021.
- Cut average deploy time from 23 minutes to 4 minutes through the Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipeline I designed.
  - *Source:* Internal deploy log, December 2020.
- Onboarded 3 incoming engineers as headcount grew from 4 to 14.
  - *Source:* Internal HR records and onboarding tracker.
