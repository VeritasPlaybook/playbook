### 4.1 Backend Infrastructure / Developer Platform Archetype

**Tags:** `#Backend` `#Infrastructure` `#Platform` `#Go` `#Distributed`
**Use when:** applying to a backend infrastructure or developer-platform company (Cloudflare, Fly.io, Vercel, PlanetScale, Sentry, Datadog, Linear, and similar). These companies value technical-depth signal and a clean code portfolio.

---

#### Opener variants

- "If you are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer who has owned a 1.2-million-events-per-minute Kafka pipeline and shipped exactly-once semantics in production, I'm your candidate for the Staff Engineer role."
- "If you are looking for a backend engineer who has led a zero-downtime Postgres-to-Citus migration and shipped the first Python Software Development Kit (SDK) at a streaming-data company, I'm your candidate."

---

#### Reason headers used in this archetype

- **"Distributed systems primitives are the work I want to keep doing."**
  Elaboration: At Beacon Pay I shipped idempotency primitives that cut duplicate-charge incidents by ~95 percent. At Lumen Streams I shipped exactly-once semantics via checkpointing. These are not buzzwords on my resume; they are the primitives I design around weekly.

- **"Zero-downtime migration is a discipline, not a luck event."**
  Elaboration: The Postgres-to-Citus migration I led at Beacon Pay hit a 7 times throughput improvement with zero customer-facing impact over a 4-month staged cutover. The discipline came from the 12 dry-run rehearsals we ran in staging before any production change.

- **"I write Request-for-Comments (RFCs) because written decisions outlive the meeting."**
  Elaboration: I authored 5 RFCs at Beacon Pay; 3 became core platform patterns adopted across adjacent teams. Written technical decisions are how I work, and they are also how I want to lead at the Staff level.

---

#### Closing variants

- "If you'd like to walk through any of the above, I'd welcome the conversation. My GitHub is https://github.com/alexrivera-eng; the retry-backoff library is the cleanest single thing to look at."

---

#### Notes and variants

- Tone: technical-depth signal first, code-portfolio second. Avoid prose-heavy bullets. Hiring managers in this archetype skim for primitives.
- Lead with the most-specific scale credibility (Beacon Pay) and the language match (Go for most companies in this archetype).
- Numbers help, but only the ones that signal scale or discipline. "Zero customer-facing downtime over a 4-month rollout" lands better than "shipped a migration".
- The GitHub link must be in the cover letter, not just on the resume. The reader will probably look.

Variant framings specifically used in this archetype:

- "Built idempotency primitives for the Beacon Pay payment Application Programming Interface (API); duplicate-charge incidents dropped ~95 percent in the 12 months post-launch. The primitives are now reused across the refund and dispute services." [Application to a Cloudflare-style Staff role; reused in two later applications]
- "Led a zero-downtime Postgres-to-Citus migration; 7 times throughput improvement over a 4-month staged cutover." [Application to a PlanetScale-style role; reused in three later applications]
