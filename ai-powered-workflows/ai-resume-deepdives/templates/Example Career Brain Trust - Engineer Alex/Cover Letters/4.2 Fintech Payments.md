### 4.2 Fintech / Payments Infrastructure Archetype

**Tags:** `#FinTech` `#Payments` `#Go` `#Distributed` `#Idempotency` `#Compliance`
**Use when:** applying to a fintech or payments-infrastructure company (Stripe, Modern Treasury, Mercury, Adyen, Plaid, and similar). These companies value payments-specific credibility (idempotency, reconciliation, multi-acquirer routing) and on-call discipline.

---

#### Opener variants

- "If you are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer who has shipped idempotency primitives for a payment Application Programming Interface (API) and built a multi-acquirer routing service, I'm your candidate for the Staff Engineer role."
- "If you are looking for a backend engineer who has cut duplicate-charge incidents by ~95 percent and handles 4 million transactions per day across 4 regions, I'm your candidate."

---

#### Reason headers used in this archetype

- **"Payments-specific primitives are the work I've been doing for three years."**
  Elaboration: At Beacon Pay I own the transaction-processing service handling roughly 4 million transactions per day. The idempotency primitives I shipped cut duplicate-charge incidents by ~95 percent. The multi-acquirer routing service I built selects between 5 acquirers based on cost and acceptance signals, and the routing logic now informs the cross-border currency routing service.

- **"Zero-downtime migration as a discipline."**
  Elaboration: The Postgres-to-Citus migration I led at Beacon Pay hit a 7 times throughput improvement with zero customer-facing impact over a 4-month staged cutover. In payments, the cost of even a 30-second visible outage during a migration is high enough that the discipline is the work.

- **"On-call rotation is part of the job, not an exception to it."**
  Elaboration: I have been on rotation continuously for 6 years across three employers. I have participated in 47 incident retrospectives. The retrospectives I have written at Beacon Pay are public on my GitHub because the next person on-call should not have to discover what I already discovered.

---

#### Closing variants

- "If you'd like to walk through the routing service architecture or the migration rehearsal pattern, I'd welcome the conversation. My GitHub is https://github.com/alexrivera-eng."

---

#### Notes and variants

- Tone: payments-credibility first, distributed-systems second. Fintech hiring managers want to know you understand both the primitives and the cost of getting them wrong.
- Lead with Beacon Pay. Lumen Streams is supporting (streaming-data primitives transfer to payments reconciliation pipelines).
- Mention on-call discipline explicitly. Fintech companies care about who picks up the page at 3am.
- Numbers help. Specific transaction volumes and specific acquirer counts land better than generic "high scale" language.

Variant framings specifically used in this archetype:

- "Designed and shipped idempotency primitives for the Beacon Pay payment Application Programming Interface (API), reducing duplicate-charge incidents by ~95 percent." [Application to a Modern-Treasury-style Staff role; reused in two later applications]
- "Built a payment routing service in Go that dynamically selects between 5 acquirers based on cost and acceptance signals; the routing logic now informs the cross-border currency routing service." [Application to a payments-orchestration role; reused in one later application]
