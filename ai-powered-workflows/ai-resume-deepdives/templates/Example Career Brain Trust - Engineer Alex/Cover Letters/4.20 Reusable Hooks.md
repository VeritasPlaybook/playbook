### 4.20 Reusable Hooks (Alex's library)

The library of openers, technical-depth signals, code-portfolio anchors, and closings that have landed across multiple applications.

---

#### Openers

- "If you are looking for a backend engineer who has shipped idempotency primitives for a payment Application Programming Interface (API) and led a zero-downtime Postgres-to-Citus migration, I'm your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#Backend` `#Payments` `#Distributed`
  **Use when:** the role is at a fintech or payments-infrastructure company.

- "If you are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer who has owned a 1.2-million-events-per-minute Kafka pipeline and shipped exactly-once semantics in production, I'm your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#Streaming` `#Distributed`
  **Use when:** the role is at a streaming-data or messaging-infrastructure company.

- "If you are looking for a backend engineer who has shipped both the first Python Software Development Kit (SDK) at one company and the platform standard idempotency primitives at another, I'm your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#DeveloperTools` `#Platform` `#SDK`
  **Use when:** the role is at a developer-platform company where Software Development Kit ownership matters.

---

#### Technical-depth signals

- **Idempotency and reconciliation:** "At Beacon Pay I shipped idempotency primitives for the payment Application Programming Interface (API); duplicate-charge incidents dropped ~95 percent in the 12 months post-launch."
  **Tags:** `#Idempotency` `#Payments`

- **Zero-downtime migration:** "Led the Postgres-to-Citus migration at Beacon Pay; 7 times throughput improvement, zero customer-facing downtime over a 4-month staged cutover."
  **Tags:** `#Migration` `#Postgres` `#Citus`

- **Exactly-once semantics:** "At Lumen Streams I implemented exactly-once semantics for downstream consumers via checkpointing primitives. The pattern is now the team's standard for new streaming services."
  **Tags:** `#ExactlyOnce` `#Streaming`

- **Service mesh migration:** "Led the migration from Google Remote Procedure Call (gRPC) on bare metal to a containerized service mesh; 99th-percentile (p99) latency dropped ~38 percent."
  **Tags:** `#ServiceMesh` `#Migration`

- **Software Development Kit (SDK) ownership:** "Wrote the first Python SDK at Lumen Streams; 4 partner integrations in the first year of general availability. Single-import surface, async-first."
  **Tags:** `#SDK` `#DeveloperTools`

- **Request-for-Comments authorship:** "Shepherded 5 RFCs at Beacon Pay; 3 became core platform patterns. Written technical decisions are how I work."
  **Tags:** `#RFC` `#Senior`

- **Mentorship at the Staff level:** "Mentored 2 mid-level engineers at Beacon Pay through their first RFCs. Office hours grew from 2 to 8 regulars. Mentorship is part of the Staff role I want, not a sidecar."
  **Tags:** `#Mentor` `#Staff`

---

#### Code portfolio anchors

- "My GitHub is the lowest-friction way to see how I actually write code: https://github.com/alexrivera-eng. The retry-backoff library I maintain is the cleanest single thing to look at."

- "If you want to see the kind of code I write today, my GitHub has both production-style work (the retry-backoff library) and exploratory work (the idempotency-primitives sketch in Rust)."

#### Closing lines

- "If you'd like to walk through any of the above, I'd welcome the conversation. My GitHub is [URL]."
