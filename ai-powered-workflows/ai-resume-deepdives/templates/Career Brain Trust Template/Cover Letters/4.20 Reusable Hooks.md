### 4.20 Reusable Hooks (modular building blocks)

This is the library of reusable phrases, openers, reason blocks, and credibility lines you've discovered work across multiple cover letters. The update-brain-trust skill adds to this file when a phrase recurs in two or more applications.

Each entry has the same shape: the phrase or block, the tags it answers to, and a one-line note on when to use it.

---

#### Openers

The "If you are looking for..." opener bank. One per role archetype.

- "If you are looking for [a builder who has shipped X in Y industry], I am your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#Build` `#[Domain]`
  **Use when:** the Job Description leads with "builder" or "founding team" language.
- "If you are looking for [a leader who has scaled X teams to Y outcomes], I am your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#Leadership` `#Scale`
  **Use when:** the Job Description is a senior leadership role at a scaled organization.
- "If you are looking for [a specialist who has owned X in regulated environments], I am your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#Compliance` `#Regulated`
  **Use when:** the Job Description names regulatory, compliance, or risk language.

Add more as you discover them.

---

#### Breadth blocks

Short paragraphs that demonstrate range. Useful when the Job Description names breadth or cross-functional work.

- **Cross-functional range:** "[Sample paragraph showing you have worked across engineering, design, data, and go-to-market.]"
  **Tags:** `#CrossFunctional` `#Breadth`

- **Stage range:** "[Sample paragraph showing you have worked from zero-to-one through scale.]"
  **Tags:** `#0to1` `#Scale`

---

#### Credibility blocks

Short paragraphs that establish credibility in a specific domain.

- **Domain X credibility:** "[Sample paragraph that signals depth in your strongest domain. Should mention a specific employer, a specific outcome, and a number if possible.]"
  **Tags:** `#[Domain X]` `#Credibility`

- **Domain Y credibility:** "[Sample paragraph in your second domain.]"
  **Tags:** `#[Domain Y]` `#Credibility`

#### Closing lines

Variants on the closing sentence. Most reusable hooks tend to be in the body; closings are often less differentiated. Still useful to have a small library.

- "If you feel I would be an asset to [team], please reach out; I would love to discuss how I can add value."
