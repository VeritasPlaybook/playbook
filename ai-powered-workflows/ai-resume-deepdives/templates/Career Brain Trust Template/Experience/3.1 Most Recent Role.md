#### Canonical Bullets

These are the verified, defensible bullets for this role. Each one is a specific accomplishment with a clear shape: verb, object, scope, result.

- [Verb] [object] [scope or context], [result or metric where possible].

Notes on canonical bullets:

- One concrete verb per bullet (built, shipped, led, designed, drove, migrated).
- Avoid hedge words (helped, supported, contributed to). If you only helped, leave it out.
- A specific number beats a generic claim every time.
- Keep bullets to one or two lines. If a bullet runs three lines, it is doing two things; split it.
