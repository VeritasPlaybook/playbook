# 7. Education

Degrees, certifications, awards, languages, and public profiles in one place. Your resume-builder skill pulls from here when an application calls for credentials.

A note on lapsed certifications: if a certification has expired, frame it in past tense ("formerly [Certification]-certified, [Start Year] to [End Year]"). Never frame a lapsed certification as if it were current.
