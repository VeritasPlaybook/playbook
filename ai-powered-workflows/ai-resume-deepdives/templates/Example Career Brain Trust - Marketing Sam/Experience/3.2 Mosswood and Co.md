### 3.2 Mosswood & Co., Marketing Manager, Brand and Retail

**Dates:** June 2020 to February 2023
**Location:** Knoxville, Tennessee
**Tags:** `#Marketing` `#Brand` `#Retail` `#CPG` `#Broadcast` `#Print` `#InStore` `#RetailPartnerships` `#Manager`
**Source priority:** LinkedIn profile (canonical, May 2026). Small-batch coffee brand; team of 3 marketers across the company.

---

#### Canonical Bullets

- Owned the integrated 2022 holiday campaign across broadcast (regional radio), print (in-store and consumer magazine), in-store activation (250 retail accounts), and email; the campaign drove Mosswood's highest in-store traffic week since 2019.
- Negotiated and launched a national retail partnership with a 600-store grocery chain; the partnership represented Mosswood's first national distribution and shipped within 8 months of the initial contact.
- Led the relaunch of the in-store collateral program across roughly 250 independent coffee-shop accounts; retail Net Promoter Score (NPS) at those accounts rose by 12 points within 12 months.
- Consolidated the agency roster from 4 agencies to 2; reduced agency fees by 18 percent year-over-year while increasing campaign output by 22 percent.
- Coached 1 incoming Marketing Coordinator through their first solo campaign; the Coordinator was promoted to Marketing Manager within 18 months.

#### Variant Framings

#### Quantified Wins

- Highest in-store traffic week since 2019 from the 2022 holiday campaign
- 600-store national grocery distribution launched within 8 months
- 12-point Net Promoter Score lift at ~250 independent retail accounts
- 18 percent year-over-year reduction in agency fees with 22 percent more campaign output
- 1 direct report coached to a promotion within 18 months
