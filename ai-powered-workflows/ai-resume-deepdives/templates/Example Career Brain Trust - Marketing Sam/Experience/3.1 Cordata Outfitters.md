### 3.1 Cordata Outfitters, Senior Marketing Manager, Lifecycle and Direct-to-Consumer

**Dates:** March 2023 to Present
**Location:** Asheville, North Carolina
**Tags:** `#Marketing` `#Lifecycle` `#DTC` `#Retail` `#Mobile` `#Email` `#Push` `#Growth` `#PLG` `#Senior` `#Build`
**Source priority:** LinkedIn profile (canonical, May 2026). Promoted from Marketing Manager to Senior Marketing Manager in September 2024.

---

#### Canonical Bullets

- Built and own the direct-to-consumer (DTC) mobile-app lifecycle program from zero; the program now drives roughly 18 percent of the company's quarterly Direct-to-Consumer mix, up from a launch baseline of 0 percent.
- Lifted month-one mobile retention by 28 percent through a re-sequenced onboarding flow plus a behaviorally-triggered push notification series, validated against the November 2024 baseline cohort.
- Grew the mobile app from launch to roughly 180,000 monthly active users (MAU) in 28 months through coordinated lifecycle, paid social, and retail-store partnership channels.
- Ran 41 A/B tests across mobile lifecycle, email, and paid social in the last 18 months; 16 produced statistically meaningful wins folded into the canonical lifecycle program.
- Built the lifecycle attribution model the broader marketing team now uses for evaluating new-channel investments.
- Coached 2 mid-level marketers through their first full lifecycle campaigns; both have since been promoted to Senior Marketing Manager roles.
- Partnered with Product and Engineering on the mobile-app onboarding redesign, including the activation-event definition that anchored the retention work.
