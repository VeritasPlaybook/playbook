### 3.3 Aldercroft Media, Account Executive (promoted from Account Coordinator)

**Dates:** August 2018 to May 2020
**Location:** Nashville, Tennessee
**Tags:** `#Marketing` `#Agency` `#Campaigns` `#CPG` `#Retail` `#Coordinator` `#Foundational`
**Source priority:** LinkedIn profile (canonical, May 2026). First role post-graduation. Promoted from Account Coordinator to Account Executive in July 2019.

---

#### Canonical Bullets

- Managed 6 Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) and retail accounts representing roughly $2.4 million in annual agency revenue.
- Led campaign execution for a regional retail client whose campaign was named "Campaign of the Year" by the Regional Marketing Association in 2020.
- Authored creative briefs for 18 campaigns across the 2 years; the brief discipline (one-page, written-and-defended, clear hypothesis) is the foundation I still use.
- Coordinated cross-functional production across creative, media, and account-management teams; ran the weekly campaign status meeting for the agency's 4 largest accounts.
