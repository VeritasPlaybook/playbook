## Section 2B: Data Discrepancy Resolutions

### Discrepancy 1

- **Question:** Was the Cordata Outfitters title "Senior Marketing Manager" at hire or after promotion?
- **Resolution:** Hired as Marketing Manager (Mar 2023), promoted to Senior Marketing Manager in September 2024. Resume should reflect both stages under the same dated block.
- **Source:** PDF of original offer letter (February 2023) and promotion email (September 2024).
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-21.

## Section 2C: Chronology Rules

- For any application, lead with Cordata Outfitters. The Mosswood & Co. and Aldercroft Media chapters are supporting context unless the role is explicitly at a traditional retail or agency company.
- The Aldercroft Media Coordinator-to-Account-Executive promotion appears as a single dated block on the resume, with both titles listed.
- Always list dates as "Month YYYY" format. Avoid abbreviated ranges that hide the recent promotion.
