# 5. Skills (Sam)

Aggregated skill inventory across the pivot. The traditional-marketing skills are still load-bearing for roles at hybrid retail-digital companies; the digital and Product-Led Growth (PLG) skills are the screen-in for pure Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) roles.

---

## Section 5A: Traditional Marketing

- Brand Strategy and Positioning
- Integrated Campaign Planning (broadcast, print, in-store, digital)
- Broadcast Media Planning (radio and television)
- Print Production (consumer magazines, in-store collateral, point-of-purchase)
- In-Store Activation Design
- Retail Partnership Management (regional and national accounts)
- Agency Management (managing creative and media agencies)
- Public Relations Coordination
- Sponsorship and Event Marketing

---

## Section 5B: Digital and Lifecycle Marketing

- Email Lifecycle Programs (welcome, onboarding, win-back, re-engagement)
- Push Notification Strategy (transactional and behavioral)
- In-App Messaging
- Mobile App Marketing (Apple App Store and Google Play Store)
- Paid Social (Meta, TikTok)
- Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Basics
- Search Engine Marketing (SEM) Basics
- Influencer Partnerships
- Affiliate Program Setup

---

## Section 5C: Product-Led Growth

- Onboarding Flow Design
- Activation Event Definition
- Retention Loop Design
- Freemium-to-Paid Conversion Strategy
- In-Product Messaging
- User Cohort Analysis

---

## Section 5D: Measurement and Experimentation

- A/B Testing (design, statistical sanity-checks, written postmortems)
- Mobile Attribution (multi-touch attribution models, last-click for sanity)
- Lifecycle Attribution Modeling
- Cohort Analysis
- Funnel Analysis
- Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Definition and Tracking

---

## Section 5E: Leadership

- Cross-Functional Alignment with Product, Engineering, Design, Customer Success
- Coaching Mid-Level Marketers (2 to date at Cordata)
- Budget Ownership (campaign-level and channel-level)
- Quarterly Planning
- Executive Stakeholder Communication

---

## Section 5F: Tools and Platforms

- Klaviyo (email and Short Message Service)
- Iterable (email and push)
- Braze (email, push, in-app messaging; current at Cordata)
- HubSpot (Customer Relationship Management plus marketing automation)
- Mailchimp (legacy at Mosswood)
- Mixpanel (event analytics)
- Google Analytics 4
- AppsFlyer (mobile attribution)
- Figma (review and feedback level, not author level)
- Notion (planning and documentation)
- Slack
- Claude and ChatGPT (current Artificial Intelligence (AI) tooling for copy review and lifecycle ideation)

---

## Section 5G: Methodologies and Frameworks

- Jobs-To-Be-Done (applied to lifecycle copy briefs)
- AARRR (Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue) Funnel
- North Star Framework
- Brand Pyramid (traditional brand-planning framework)
- Integrated Marketing Communications (the textbook framework, still useful)
- Retention Sequencing (a Reforge framework I lean on for mobile lifecycle)

---

## Section 5H: Languages (Spoken)

- English: Native
- French: Conversational
