### 4.20 Reusable Hooks (Sam's library)

The library of openers, brand-to-growth bridges, traditional-to-digital translation hooks, and closings that have landed across multiple applications.

---

#### Openers

- "I'm three years into a deliberate pivot from traditional brand-and-retail marketing into lifecycle and Product-Led Growth (PLG). The pivot is real, recent, and earned. Here's what I think you'll find useful."
  **Tags:** `#SaaS` `#PLG` `#Pivot`
  **Use when:** the role is at a Software-as-a-Service or pure Product-Led Growth company.

- "I came up in brand and retail marketing and pivoted into lifecycle and Product-Led Growth over the last three years. The brand foundation is what I think makes the lifecycle work; here's how that shows up at Cordata Outfitters."
  **Tags:** `#DTC` `#Retail` `#Transformation`
  **Use when:** the role is at a hybrid retail-digital or Direct-to-Consumer company where the traditional roots are a feature.

- "If you are looking for a Senior Marketing Manager who has shipped a Direct-to-Consumer mobile app from launch to roughly 180,000 monthly active users and lifted month-one retention by 28 percent, I'm your candidate for the Director of Marketing role."
  **Tags:** `#PLG` `#Mobile` `#Lifecycle`
  **Use when:** the role is at a mobile-app-first company and the pivot is less prominent in the JD.

---

#### Brand-to-growth bridges

- **Brand foundation under lifecycle work:** "Emails that convert and push notifications that retain are not separate from the brand. They are the brand showing up in 60-character form. The brand work I did at Mosswood & Co. is the foundation under the lifecycle work I do at Cordata Outfitters."
  **Tags:** `#Bridge` `#Brand` `#Lifecycle`

- **Integrated thinking translates to multi-channel growth:** "Integrated marketing across broadcast, print, in-store, and email at Mosswood taught me that the customer does not separate channels; they experience the brand. That same lesson is what makes the lifecycle attribution model I built at Cordata work."
  **Tags:** `#Bridge` `#IntegratedCampaigns` `#Attribution`

- **Brief discipline as the operating system:** "The brief discipline I came up with at Aldercroft Media (one-page, written-and-defended, clear hypothesis) is the same discipline behind the experiment briefs I write at Cordata for every A/B test."
  **Tags:** `#Bridge` `#Discipline` `#Experimentation`

---

#### Reason headers

- **"I've shipped the Direct-to-Consumer mobile-app lifecycle program at Cordata from zero."**
  **Tags:** `#PLG` `#Mobile` `#Lifecycle`
  **Use when:** the JD names mobile lifecycle, app marketing, or zero-to-one program ownership.

- **"I built the lifecycle attribution model Cordata's broader marketing team now uses."**
  **Tags:** `#Attribution` `#Measurement`
  **Use when:** the JD names marketing measurement, attribution, or growth analytics.

- **"I ran 41 A/B tests in the last 18 months."**
  **Tags:** `#ABTesting` `#Experimentation`
  **Use when:** the JD names experimentation cadence or growth experimentation.

- **"Brand foundation is the lever under every lifecycle decision I make."**
  **Tags:** `#Brand` `#Bridge`
  **Use when:** the JD is at a brand-led Software-as-a-Service company or a Direct-to-Consumer company that values both halves.

- **"I'm a Senior Manager today; I want a Director chair where coaching the team is treated as the work."**
  **Tags:** `#Leadership` `#Director`
  **Use when:** the JD is Director of Marketing or Head of Growth and team-building readiness is the bar.

---

#### Closing lines

- "If you'd like to talk through how the brand foundation and the lifecycle work both show up on day one, I'd welcome the conversation."
- "If there's a fit, I'd love to walk you through the attribution model and the 41-test experiment log."
