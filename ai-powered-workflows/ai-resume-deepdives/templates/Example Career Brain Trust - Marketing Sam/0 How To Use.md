# 0. How To Use This Career Brain Trust (Sam's notes)

Short operating manual.

---

## What this Brain Trust is

A small filing cabinet of evergreen content about my marketing career. It serves two parallel goals:

1. **Preserve my traditional-marketing canonical wins.** Brand strategy, broadcast and print campaigns, retail partnerships, in-store activations. These are real wins; they took years to build; they translate.
2. **Build pivot evidence into the source of truth.** Email lifecycle, push notifications, in-app messaging, growth experimentation, mobile app marketing, Product-Led Growth (PLG) loops. These are the newer wins from the Cordata Outfitters role and from the Reforge curriculum I am working through.

The pivot is the through-line of the Brain Trust. Every Experience file has both a traditional-marketing column and a digital-marketing column. Cover letters lead with whichever side the Job Description (JD) screens for.

## Maintenance habits

**After every application:** run the update-brain-trust skill. For Sam specifically, pay attention to any new pivot-evidence framing that lands. Promote it fast.

**Weekly review:** ten minutes. Scan the variant framings from the week. If a digital or Product-Led Growth (PLG) framing has been used in two applications and resonated, promote it to a canonical bullet on the relevant Experience file.

**Quarterly audit:** every three months, re-read `1 Identity.md`. The pivot direction will move. Six months ago the headline emphasized "brand-roots growth marketer." Today it emphasizes "lifecycle and Product-Led Growth at Cordata." In six more months, it may emphasize "Director of Marketing or Head of Growth." Update the headline as the pivot lands.

**Pivot framing rule:** for any Software-as-a-Service or Product-Led Growth application, name the pivot in the opening. Hiding it reads worse than naming it.
