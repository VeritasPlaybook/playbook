# 1. Identity (Sam)

---

## Section 1A: LinkedIn Headline

> **Senior Marketing Manager building direct-to-consumer growth at Cordata Outfitters | broadcast and retail roots, lifecycle and Product-Led Growth (PLG) now | targeting Director of Marketing or Head of Growth**

Notes:

- The headline names both halves of the pivot explicitly: traditional roots (broadcast, retail) and the current direction (lifecycle, PLG).
- "Building direct-to-consumer growth at Cordata Outfitters" front-loads the most recent and most-pivot-relevant work.
- The target ("Director of Marketing or Head of Growth") is explicit so recruiters do not waste my time on Senior Manager openings.
- Last refresh: 2026-05-21.

---

## Section 1B: Professional Summary

> I'm a Senior Marketing Manager at Cordata Outfitters, where I built and now own the direct-to-consumer (D2C) mobile-app lifecycle program from zero. The work is a deliberate pivot from the brand-and-retail marketing I came up through at Mosswood & Co. and Aldercroft Media; the transferable lesson across both halves of my career has been that any growth motion lives or dies on the integrated story behind it.
>
> In three years at Cordata I've grown app-driven Direct-to-Consumer revenue from a launch number to a meaningful share of the company's quarterly mix, lifted month-one mobile retention by 28 percent, and built the lifecycle attribution model the broader team now uses for new-channel investments. I'm looking for a Director of Marketing or Head of Growth role where the brand foundation and the Product-Led Growth playbook are both treated as load-bearing, not as separate languages spoken on different floors.

## Section 1C: Positioning Library

### Brand-to-growth bridge framing

> "I came up in brand and retail and pivoted into lifecycle and Product-Led Growth (PLG) deliberately, not by accident. The brand foundation is what makes the lifecycle work; emails that convert and push notifications that retain are not separate from the brand. They are the brand showing up in 60-character form."
**Tags:** `#Pivot` `#Brand` `#PLG` `#Bridge`
**Use when:** the role is at a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) brand or a Software-as-a-Service company that values brand and growth working together, not as separate functions.

### Pivot acknowledged framing

> "I'm three years into a deliberate pivot from traditional brand and retail marketing into lifecycle and Product-Led Growth. The pivot is real, recent, and earned: I built the mobile-app lifecycle program at Cordata Outfitters from zero, completed Reforge Growth Marketing and Reforge Retention and Engagement, and I'm ready for the Director-of-Marketing or Head-of-Growth chair."
**Tags:** `#Pivot` `#CareerChange` `#PLG`
**Use when:** the role is at a Software-as-a-Service or pure Product-Led Growth company where the reader will spot the pivot and want me to name it explicitly.

### Lifecycle and retention framing

> "I lifted month-one mobile retention by 28 percent at Cordata through a re-sequenced onboarding flow plus a behaviorally-triggered push notification series. The win came from treating retention as a sequencing problem rather than a copy problem."
**Tags:** `#Lifecycle` `#Retention` `#Mobile`
**Use when:** the role is a Lifecycle Marketing or Retention Lead position where the Job Description (JD) explicitly names retention metrics.

### Brand strategy framing

> "I owned the integrated campaign for Mosswood & Co.'s 2022 holiday season across broadcast, print, in-store, and email. The campaign drove the highest in-store traffic week the brand had recorded since 2019 and was Mosswood's first holiday push that ran in lockstep across all four channels."
**Tags:** `#Brand` `#IntegratedCampaigns` `#Retail`
**Use when:** the role is at a brand-led company or a hybrid retail-digital company where brand chops are the screen-in signal.

### Integrated channels framing

> "I've planned and executed across broadcast, print, in-store, retail partnerships, email, push notifications, in-app messaging, paid social, and Search Engine Optimization (SEO). The constant across all of them is that the customer does not separate channels; they just experience the brand."
**Tags:** `#IntegratedCampaigns` `#CrossChannel` `#Breadth`
**Use when:** the role is at a company where omnichannel is the explicit strategy.

### Leadership framing

> "I'm a Senior Manager today and I've coached 2 mid-level marketers at Cordata into running their own campaigns. I want a Director-level role where coaching the team is treated as the work, not as something I do in between campaigns."
**Tags:** `#Leadership` `#Director` `#HeadOfGrowth`
**Use when:** the role is Director of Marketing or Head of Growth and the JD names team-building or coaching readiness.

### Experimentation framing

> "I've run roughly 40 A/B tests in the last 18 months across the mobile app, the email program, and the paid-social channel at Cordata. The discipline that came out of brand planning (clear hypothesis, predicted outcome, written postmortem) is what makes the experimentation cadence actually useful, not just busy."
**Tags:** `#ABTesting` `#Experimentation` `#Growth`
**Use when:** the role is at a growth-experimentation-heavy company.

### Mobile-app framing

> "I own the lifecycle program for the Cordata Outfitters mobile app: onboarding, activation, retention, win-back. The app went from launch to roughly 180,000 monthly active users in 28 months on the lifecycle programs I designed."
**Tags:** `#Mobile` `#PLG` `#Lifecycle`
**Use when:** the role is at a mobile-app-first company or at a consumer-tech company where mobile lifecycle is the screen-in.
