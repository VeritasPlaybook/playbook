# 6. Achievements (Sam)

---

## Cordata Outfitters

- Grew app-driven Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) revenue from launch to roughly 18 percent of the company's quarterly Direct-to-Consumer mix within 24 months.
  - *Source:* Internal quarterly business review report, Q1 2026.
- Lifted month-one mobile retention by 28 percent through a re-sequenced onboarding flow plus a behaviorally-triggered push notification series.
  - *Source:* Internal Mixpanel cohort report, November 2024 vs. May 2025.
- Grew the mobile app from launch to roughly 180,000 monthly active users (MAU) in 28 months.
  - *Source:* AppsFlyer reporting dashboard, snapshot April 2026.
- Ran 41 A/B tests across mobile lifecycle, email, and paid social in the last 18 months; 16 produced statistically meaningful wins that were rolled into the canonical lifecycle program.
  - *Source:* Internal experiment log, snapshot May 2026.
- Built the lifecycle attribution model the broader marketing team now uses for evaluating new-channel investments.
  - *Source:* Internal documentation in the marketing-ops Notion workspace.

---

## Mosswood & Co.

- Drove Mosswood's highest in-store traffic week since 2019 through the 2022 holiday integrated campaign (broadcast, print, in-store, email).
  - *Source:* Retail partner traffic data, December 2022 vs. December 2019.
- Negotiated and launched a national retail partnership with a 600-store grocery chain; the partnership represented Mosswood's first national distribution.
  - *Source:* Signed distribution agreement, February 2022; partnership press release.
- Lifted retail Net Promoter Score (NPS) at independent coffee-shop accounts by 12 points through a relaunch of the in-store collateral program.
  - *Source:* Internal NPS survey, Q4 2021 vs. Q4 2022.
- Reduced agency fees by 18 percent year-over-year through agency-roster consolidation while increasing campaign output by 22 percent.
  - *Source:* Internal marketing finance report, year-end 2022.

  - *Source:* Industry award press release, March 2020.
