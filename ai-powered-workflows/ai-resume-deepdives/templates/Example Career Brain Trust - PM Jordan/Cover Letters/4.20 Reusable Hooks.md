### 4.20 Reusable Hooks (Jordan's library)

The library of openers, reason headlines, and closing lines that have landed across multiple applications. The update-brain-trust skill adds to this file after each application where a phrasing recurs.

---

#### Openers

- "If you are looking for a Senior Product Manager (PM) who has shipped developer workflow tooling used by 12,000-plus engineers and knows when to let the Artificial Intelligence (AI) act vs. ask, I'm your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#DeveloperTools` `#Platform` `#AI`
  **Use when:** the role is a developer-facing platform role at a company with an AI surface.

- "If you are looking for a PM who has shipped inside a regulated vertical Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) environment and knows that compliance and usability are the same workstream, I'm your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#VerticalSaaS` `#Compliance` `#HIPAA`
  **Use when:** the role is in vertical SaaS, particularly healthcare, legal, or financial services.

- "If you are looking for a Lead PM who has both built from zero-to-one and operated at platform scale, I'm your candidate."
  **Tags:** `#Lead` `#Build` `#Scale`
  **Use when:** the role is a Lead PM at a mid-stage company where both modes are required.

---

#### Breadth blocks

- **Cross-functional range:** "I've shipped with Engineering inside Tessera, with Engineering plus Compliance plus Privacy inside Brightline Health Systems, and with Engineering plus Sales-Engineering inside Quantile Insights. The constants: discovery happens with the team, not at the team."
  **Tags:** `#CrossFunctional` `#Breadth`

- **Stage range:** "I've owned features at zero-to-one (Quantile Insights natural-language query), at the change-management seam (Brightline clinician inbox redesign), and at platform scale (Tessera workflow automation engine adopted by ~70 percent of paid customers within 90 days)."
  **Tags:** `#0to1` `#Scale` `#StageRange`

---

#### Credibility blocks

- **Developer workflow credibility:** "At Tessera I own the developer workflow surface. 12,000-plus engineers across 200-plus engineering organizations use what I ship. I run a customer council with engineering leads at 8 high-Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) accounts because the best roadmap comes from people who break the product before I do."
  **Tags:** `#DeveloperTools` `#Credibility`

- **Vertical SaaS credibility (healthcare):** "At Brightline Health Systems I cut clinician documentation time by roughly 40 percent across 4 ambulatory care pilots and grew the Electronic Health Records (EHR) integration module from 0 to 23 supported systems in 18 months. In healthcare, trust is the product. I learned to ship inside that constraint."
  **Tags:** `#VerticalSaaS` `#Healthcare` `#Credibility`

- **AI product judgment credibility:** "The AI task assistant inside Tessera lifted week-one retention by 31 percent. The lift came from the product decision (when to act vs. when to ask), not the model choice. I'm interested in roles where that distinction matters."
  **Tags:** `#AI` `#Product` `#Credibility`

---

#### Reason headers

- **"I ship for working engineering teams under load, not demo conditions."**
  **Tags:** `#DeveloperTools` `#Build`
  **Use when:** the JD names "build for real users" or anti-demoware language.

- **"Trust is the product in vertical Software-as-a-Service."**
  **Tags:** `#VerticalSaaS` `#Trust`
  **Use when:** the JD is in vertical SaaS or healthcare or legal or finance.

- **"Integrations breadth is the expansion lever."**
  **Tags:** `#Integrations` `#Platform`
  **Use when:** the JD names integrations partnerships, ecosystem strategy, or platform expansion.

- **"I'm a senior individual contributor today; I want a Lead role where I can mentor with explicit ownership."**
  **Tags:** `#Leadership` `#Lead` `#Pivot`
  **Use when:** the role is Lead PM or Group PM and seniority/leadership readiness is the bar.

---

#### Closing lines

- "If you'd like to talk through where I could plug in fastest, I'd welcome the conversation."
- "If there's a fit, I'd love to compare notes on what 'good' looks like for this team."
