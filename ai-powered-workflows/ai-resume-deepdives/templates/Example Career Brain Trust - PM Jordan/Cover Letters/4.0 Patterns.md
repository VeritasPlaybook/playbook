### 4.0 Recurring Patterns (Jordan's cover letter house style)

A consistent four-part skeleton across my cover letters:

1. **Opening hook.** "If you are looking for a [specific role description], I'm your candidate." Tuned per archetype.
2. **Header.** "Here's why I think there's a fit."
3. **Three or four bolded reason headlines**, each followed by a two-to-four-sentence elaboration anchored in a specific employer and a specific outcome.
4. **Closing.** "If you'd like to talk through where I could plug in fastest, I'd welcome the conversation."

Notes:

- The opener is the single highest-value sentence; I rewrite it for every application.
- The bolded reasons recur across applications inside the same archetype. They are the "Lego blocks" the resume-builder skill picks from in `4.20 Reusable Hooks.md`.
- The closing is intentionally low-key; I want the reader to leave the page thinking about the reasons, not the closing.

---

#### When I break the pattern

- When the application asks for a specific format ("tell us in 200 words why you want to work here"). I honor the format and lift the strongest two reasons into the constrained shape.
- When I have a one-of-one credibility hook ("I shipped against your developer platform from inside Tessera"). I lead with that.
