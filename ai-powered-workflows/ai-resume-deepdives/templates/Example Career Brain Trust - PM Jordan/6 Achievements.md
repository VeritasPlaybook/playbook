# 6. Achievements (Jordan)

Deduplicated headline metrics, organized by employer, with source attribution.

---

## Tessera

- Shipped real-time code review and collaboration interface used by 12,000-plus engineers across 200-plus engineering organizations.
  - *Source:* Internal "platform reach" dashboard, snapshot April 2026.
- Drove ~70 percent paid-customer adoption of the workflow automation engine within 90 days of launch.
  - *Source:* Internal adoption funnel report, Q1 2026.
- Drove a 31 percent week-one retention lift on the Artificial Intelligence (AI) task assistant feature.
  - *Source:* Internal Amplitude cohort analysis, post-launch report October 2025.
- Drove a 19 percent expansion in average accounts per paying customer through the integrations roadmap (6 new connectors: GitHub Enterprise, GitLab self-hosted, Jira Data Center, Linear, Sentry, PagerDuty).
  - *Source:* Internal account-expansion analysis, March 2026.
- Ran weekly customer councils with engineering leads at 8 high-Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) accounts; the council surfaced 4 major roadmap items that shipped within the year.
  - *Source:* Internal council minutes archive plus roadmap delivery log, 2025.

---

## Brightline Health Systems

- Reduced average clinician documentation time from 12 minutes to 7 minutes per patient (roughly 40 percent reduction), validated across 4 ambulatory care pilots.
  - *Source:* Pilot summary report, presented at internal Product Review, August 2023.
- Grew the Electronic Health Records (EHR) integration module from 0 to 23 supported systems over 18 months.
  - *Source:* Internal integrations roadmap and partner success report, October 2023.
- Drove a 38 percent rise in daily active usage of the clinician inbox post-redesign, sustained over 6 months.
  - *Source:* Mixpanel cohort analysis, snapshot August 2023.
- Cleared the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) compliance design review for patient messaging in 6 weeks vs. internal benchmark of 12 weeks.
  - *Source:* Compliance review log, internal Confluence, April 2022.

---

## Quantile Insights

- Led the natural-language query feature from zero to live beta with 50 design partner accounts within 8 months.
  - *Source:* Internal launch summary, January 2021.
- Shipped an embedded analytics Software Development Kit (SDK) consumed by 11 partner platforms within 12 months of general availability.
  - *Source:* Partner integration log, February 2021.
- Wrote the company's first internal product spec template; adopted by all 4 Product Managers and still in use across the now-9-person team as of 2026.
  - *Source:* Confluence template version history.
