### 3.1 Tessera, Senior Product Manager, Developer Workflow

**Dates:** December 2023 to Present
**Location:** Remote (Boston, Massachusetts)
**Tags:** `#Product` `#DeveloperTools` `#Platform` `#SaaS` `#AI` `#Workflow` `#Integrations` `#Build` `#Scale` `#Senior`
**Source priority:** LinkedIn profile (canonical, May 2026), promoted to Senior Product Manager (PM) in June 2025.

---

#### Canonical Bullets

- Shipped a real-time code review and collaboration interface integrated into the editor, used by 12,000-plus engineers across 200-plus engineering organizations.
- Built a workflow automation engine that lets engineering teams compose triggers, conditions, and actions; ~70 percent of paid customers adopted within their first 90 days.
- Led discovery and shipping of an Artificial Intelligence (AI)-powered task assistant for the developer workflow, contributing to a 31 percent week-one retention lift on the assistant feature.
- Owned the integrations roadmap (GitHub Enterprise, GitLab self-hosted, Jira Data Center, Linear, Sentry, PagerDuty); 6 new connectors drove a 19 percent expansion in average accounts per paying customer.
- Ran weekly customer councils with engineering leads at 8 high-Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) accounts; surfaced and prioritized 4 major roadmap items that shipped within the year.
- Mentored 3 Associate Product Managers through their first roadmap planning cycles; 2 have since been promoted to Product Manager.
- Designed and shipped the Product Manager hiring loop for the Developer Workflow team (4 hires closed in 2025).

- "Shipped real-time code review surfaces inside the developer editor, used by 12,000-plus engineers; designed for working teams under load, not demo conditions."

  *[Application to a Group Product Manager role at a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) workflow company]. Useful when adoption-curve credibility is the signal the JD is screening for.*

  *[Application to an AI-native company]. Useful when the JD asks for "AI product judgment" or "model-aware product decisions" specifically.*
- "Owned the developer-tooling integrations roadmap end-to-end; shipped 6 connectors (GitHub Enterprise, GitLab self-hosted, Jira Data Center, Linear, Sentry, PagerDuty) that drove a 19 percent expansion in average accounts per paying customer."
  *[Application to a developer-platform PM role at PlanetScale-style company]. Useful when the JD is API-first or integrations-led.*
