### 3.2 Brightline Health Systems, Product Manager, Clinician Workflow

**Dates:** March 2021 to November 2023
**Location:** Boston, Massachusetts
**Tags:** `#Product` `#VerticalSaaS` `#Healthcare` `#EHR` `#Compliance` `#HIPAA` `#Build` `#Usability`
**Source priority:** LinkedIn profile (canonical, May 2026).

---

#### Canonical Bullets

- Built a clinician documentation workflow that reduced average charting time from 12 minutes to 7 minutes per patient (roughly 40 percent reduction), validated across 4 ambulatory care pilots.
- Shipped a structured Electronic Health Records (EHR) integration module that grew from 0 to 23 supported EHR systems over 18 months, including Epic, Cerner, athenahealth, and 20 community-hospital systems.
- Owned a usability-driven redesign of the clinician inbox; daily active usage of the inbox rose 38 percent post-launch, sustained over 6 months.
- Partnered with Compliance and Privacy to ship Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)-compliant patient messaging; cleared design review in 6 weeks vs. internal benchmark of 12.
- Led discovery for the post-visit summary feature; ran 14 clinician interviews and 3 ambulatory observation sessions, producing the feature spec that shipped in Q3 2022.
- Coached 2 Associate Product Managers through their first major roadmap deliverable; both promoted to Product Manager within 18 months.
