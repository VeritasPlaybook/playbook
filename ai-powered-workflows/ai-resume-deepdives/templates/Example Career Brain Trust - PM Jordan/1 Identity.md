# 1. Identity (Jordan)

This file holds my professional identity: headline, summary, and a library of alternate positioning statements.

---

## Section 1A: LinkedIn Headline

> **Senior Product Manager building developer workflow tools | shipped from 0 to 1 across vertical Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) and platform | engineering empathy from first principles**

Notes:

- Emphasizes the current role context (developer workflow) and the breadth (vertical SaaS plus platform).
- "Engineering empathy from first principles" reads better to engineering hiring managers than generic "user-centric" or "customer-obsessed" language.
- Last refresh: 2026-05-21.

---

## Section 1B: Professional Summary

> I'm a Senior Product Manager (PM) building developer workflow tooling at Tessera. I came up through analytics and vertical SaaS, then moved to the platform layer when I realized the workflow problem was upstream of every analytics insight I'd shipped.
>
> Over six years I've taken a natural-language query feature from zero to live for 50 design partners, redesigned a clinician workflow that cut documentation time by roughly 40 percent across four ambulatory care pilots, and shipped a workflow automation engine that ~70 percent of paid customers adopt within their first 90 days. I work best with engineers who like to push back, ship in small increments, and measure what they shipped a week later.

## Section 1C: Positioning Library

### Builder framing

> "I build product zero to one with engineers, not for them. The features I'm proudest of started as a thirty-minute pairing session at a whiteboard with the lead engineer, not a polished spec dropped over the wall."
**Tags:** `#Build` `#0to1` `#Engineering`
**Use when:** the role explicitly calls for builders, founding-team experience, or "ships with engineering" language.

### Scale framing

> "I've shipped features used by 12,000-plus engineers across 200-plus engineering organizations. Knowing what breaks at that volume, especially in workflow tooling where one bad release blocks an entire team's day, has changed how I think about the small choices."
**Tags:** `#Scale` `#Platform` `#DeveloperTools`
**Use when:** the role is at a scaled developer platform or workflow product.

### Domain-specialist framing (developer tools)

> "Developer workflow is the seam where product, engineering culture, and individual contributor habits collide. I've spent the last two-plus years inside that seam and I'm fluent in the actual day of an engineer using an editor, a code review tool, and a workflow automation engine in parallel."
**Tags:** `#DeveloperTools` `#Platform` `#Expert`
**Use when:** the role is a developer-facing product role and the team wants a domain expert.

### Domain-specialist framing (vertical SaaS, healthcare)

> "I spent two and a half years shipping clinician workflow inside a regulated, change-resistant environment. The lesson that stuck: in vertical SaaS, trust is the product. Compliance and usability are not separate workstreams; they are the same workstream wearing two hats."
**Tags:** `#VerticalSaaS` `#Healthcare` `#Compliance` `#Trust`
**Use when:** the role is in vertical SaaS, regulated industries, or anywhere the buyer-to-user distance is large.

### Generalist framing

> "I've shipped analytics, vertical SaaS, and developer platform. The constants across all three: get the workflow right, the integrations breadth matters more than any individual feature, and the people who use the product daily will tell you what to build if you build the channel to listen."
**Tags:** `#Generalist` `#CrossDomain`
**Use when:** the role is at an earlier-stage company where the PM needs to wear several hats.

### Leadership framing

> "I'm a senior individual contributor today, but I've mentored three associate Product Managers through their first roadmap and led a customer council that surfaced four major roadmap items in the last year. I want a Lead or Group role where I can do that work with explicit ownership rather than as a side effect."
**Tags:** `#Leadership` `#Lead` `#Group` `#Pivot`
**Use when:** the role is Lead Product Manager or Group Product Manager and the hiring manager will look for evidence of people-leadership readiness.

### AI-in-the-loop framing

> "I shipped the AI-powered task assistant inside Tessera's workflow. The hard part wasn't the model; it was deciding when the assistant should act and when it should ask. That product decision, more than any model choice, drove the 31 percent week-one retention lift."
**Tags:** `#AI` `#LLM` `#Product`
**Use when:** the role is at an AI-native company or AI is a meaningful surface in the job description.

### Pivot framing

> "I came up through Business-to-Business (B2B) analytics and vertical SaaS, then deliberately moved to the developer platform layer at Tessera. The pivot was intentional and I'm three years in. Anything I learned in vertical SaaS still travels with me; I just use it now to ship for engineering teams rather than clinicians."
**Tags:** `#Pivot` `#CareerChange`
**Use when:** the role is a continuation of the developer-platform direction and I want to acknowledge the earlier vertical SaaS chapter rather than have the reader guess.
