# Prompt Library: What to Run and in What Order

Five prompts. Four are for a deep research tool, one is an email to a human. Together they build the intelligence layer: what you know about the company, the round, and the people, separated into what is verified and what is guessed.

You do not need all five. Most people running a normal round need three, and the honest ranking by value per minute is 05 first, then 02, then 01, then 03, then 04.

---

# The order

**Send 05 the day the round is scheduled.** Four minutes of work, and the reply tells you who is in the room, the input everything else depends on. Sending it late is the most common sequencing mistake in the whole kit.

**Then 01, once per company.** The company facts do not change between rounds, so this file is written once and reused for the whole loop.

**Then 02, once per named interviewer.** Do not batch two people into one run. This is the prompt that most needs the reply from 05, because research on a person you cannot positively identify is fabrication with extra steps.

**Then 03, once per company and level.** It reconstructs the scorecard the panel is likely using, converting your preparation from answering questions into covering competencies.

**Then 04, in a different research tool, on your load-bearing claims only.** Different vendor, not a second thread in the same tool. Running the same model twice mostly reproduces the same errors with more confidence.

---

# How many you actually need

| Your situation | Run these | Skip these | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interview is tomorrow | 05, 02 | 01, 03, 04 | You have time for one research pass. Knowing the person beats knowing the company, and 05 is free. |
| Two to three days | 05, 01, 02 | 03, 04 | Enough for a working brief and one dossier. Stay strictly inside the "do not assert" list, because nothing is double-checked. |
| Four days or more, normal round | 05, 01, 02, 03 | 04 optional | The standard build. Add 04 if the dossier contains claims you plan to say out loud. |
| Final round, executive panel, or a role you badly want | All five | Nothing | Being confidently wrong in front of an executive is the worst outcome available. Run the second pass. |
| Recruiter screen only | 05, 01 (sections one to three) | 02, 03, 04 | You are being screened on basics and interest. A full dossier is wasted effort here. |
| You only have a job title, no names | 05, 01, 03 | 02 | Without a confirmed identity, interviewer research produces plausible fiction. Spend the time on the competency model instead. |
| Same company, second or third round | 05, 02 for the new people | 01, 03 | Reuse the existing brief and competency table. Only the people changed. |

---

# Three rules that apply to all of them

**Demand the split.** Every output must separate VERIFIED (source link) from INFERRED (reasoning shown). If it hands you a blended narrative, ask again. That instruction is the difference between research and text that sounds like research.

**Treat a negative finding as a finding.** "Searched, not found" is a real answer. A tool that never fails to find anything is not searching.

**Write the substitute phrasing before you close the file.** Every unverified claim goes into a **Do not assert** list with the safe question you would ask instead. Striking a claim is not enough, because under pressure you reach for it anyway.
