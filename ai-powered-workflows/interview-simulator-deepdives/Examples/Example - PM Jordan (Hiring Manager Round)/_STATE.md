# Standing preferences

- One question per turn during a mock, then stop and wait.
- No hints. Do not tell me which story to use.
- Grade at the end of the run, not after each answer.
- I dictate. Assume garbled or misplaced words are transcription artifacts. Grade substance and intent, never how it came out on screen.
- No em dashes in anything written for me.
