## How it actually went

*Two parts. First the tone read, in your own words, not a grade. Then their question list in the order asked, as close to their real wording as you can get. The order matters: people front load what they are screening for.*

*Be blunt. Nobody else reads this. Shaky includes anything you got through but would not want asked again the same way.*

## Intel they gave away when answering your questions

*The highest value section in the file. Answering your questions, they stop performing and start describing their actual working life. Everything the next round gets built on comes from here. If you are short on time, write this first.*

- **How they described the team:** [THEIR WORDS, VERBATIM WHERE POSSIBLE]
- **The hardest problem they named:** [PROBLEM]. How they described the pain: [WORDS]
- **Words or phrases they repeated:** [WORD]: used [NUMBER] times, in the context of [CONTEXT]
- **What they seemed tired of:** [TOPIC, TOOL, PROCESS, OR TYPE OF CANDIDATE]
- **What they lit up about:** [TOPIC]
- **Anything they said about the next round:** [WHO, WHAT FORMAT, WHAT THEY WILL FOCUS ON]
- **Any name they dropped:** [NAME, CONTEXT]
- **Anything that contradicted my Company and Role Brief:** [CORRECTION, AND WHICH LINE IN THE BRIEF TO FIX]
