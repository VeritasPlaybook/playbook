# Do not assert

*The most useful section in this file. Every plausible sounding claim you have not verified goes here, with the safe phrasing you will use instead. Under pressure your brain reaches for the confident version. This list is what stops it.*

*Format for each item: the claim, why it is unverified, the safe substitute.*

1. **Claim:** [SPECIFIC CLAIM ABOUT THE COMPANY]
   **Why unverified:** [INFERRED FROM A JOB POSTING, HEARD SECONDHAND, MODEL GENERATED]
   **Safe substitute:** "[HEDGED PHRASING THAT KEEPS THE POINT WITHOUT THE FALSE FACT]"

2. **Claim:** [SPECIFIC CLAIM ABOUT THE TEAM OR ROADMAP]
   **Why unverified:** [REASON]
   **Safe substitute:** "[HEDGED PHRASING]"

3. **Claim:** [SPECIFIC CLAIM ABOUT A PERSON]
   **Why unverified:** [REASON]
   **Safe substitute:** "[HEDGED PHRASING]"

**Worked example (fictional):**

4. **Claim:** Northwind Payments is migrating its fraud scoring off a rules engine onto machine learning (ML) models this year.
   **Why unverified:** It came from one job posting mentioning "model driven risk decisions." The posting does not say the rules engine is being retired, and gives no timeline. A hiring manager who owns that engine will hear the assertion as a stranger telling them their system is dead.
   **Safe substitute:** "The posting mentions model driven risk decisions, so I read that as machine learning sitting alongside the rules. Is that right, and is the rules layer staying as the fallback?"
