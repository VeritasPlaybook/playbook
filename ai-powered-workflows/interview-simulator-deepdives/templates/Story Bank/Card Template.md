## Who did what

*Be precise about your part versus your team's versus a vendor's. Vagueness here reads as credit taking, and a good interviewer will probe it deliberately.*

- **Me:** [what you personally did]

## Honest boundary

> [The line you say when a follow up goes past what you actually did or know.]

*Something like: "I owned the design and the trade offs. The implementation of [X] was owned by [function], so I can tell you why we chose it and what we measured, but I would be guessing about the internals." Naming the edge of your knowledge reads as senior. Bluffing past it does not, and it is detectable inside one question.*
