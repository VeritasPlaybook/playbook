## Copy and paste this

Most people hitting the deep research button in their AI are getting a suboptimal run. I was one of them for a long time.

You ask a question, it disappears for ten minutes, and back comes a long report with a wall of links. Looks thorough. Then you check one of the sources and it does not quite say what the report says it does.

That is not just me being picky. A study this year found the links in these reports work over 94% of the time, but the claims match the source only 39 to 77% of the time.

What I actually wanted was a research buddy. Something that knows how deep a question deserves to go, and checks what comes back against the real sources before handing it to me.

So I built one as a Claude skill and wrote up how. The biggest saving surprised me... most of my questions never needed the deep run at all, a quick scan first was enough.

I also put it up against Hyperresearch by @Jordan Gibbs, the heaviest open source research harness I could find. Great for something like a literature review, way too much machine for a Tuesday afternoon question. I borrowed its best checks and left the rest.

It is free and it is not perfect. The article says where.

When you run deep research today, what is the part you do not trust?
