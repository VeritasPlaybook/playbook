# Image Prompts: Deep Research Skill v2 Cover (4 options)

All four use your current cover style ("The Web Is Going Headless", "Build Your Own Model Council"). None of them shows AI as a person or a face: the "buddy" is always light, glass or a tool.

**How to use:** paste the STYLE block, then ONE scene block underneath it, into ChatGPT. Try two or three and pick the one that lands.

**Shape:** 16:9. ChatGPT usually outputs 1536 x 1024, so crop to 16:9 in Canva, keeping the figure and the glow in frame.

---

## STYLE (always paste this first)

```
Create a wide landscape illustration, 16:9, for a LinkedIn article cover.

Style: soft, airy, dreamy digital illustration with a gentle watercolor and pastel feel. Warm cream and ivory background, lots of soft light and haze, fine grain texture. Pastel palette of lavender, peach, soft coral, mint green and pale sky blue. Calm, hopeful, premium editorial mood. Plenty of empty cream space. Delicate lavender stems in one lower corner.

No text, letters or numbers anywhere in the image. No logos, no robots, no faces or human shapes made of light, no glowing brains, no dark or neon colors, no harsh shadows, no 3D render look.
```

---

## Option 1: The filter (woman alone, closest to your last cover)

```
Scene: on the left, a huge messy stream of floating translucent cards drifts in on misty ribbons of color: small documents, charts, pie graphs, article snippets, dozens of them, overlapping. In the centre right, a young woman in a soft cream dress, hair in a loose bun, seen from the side, calm and focused, raises one hand toward the stream. As the cards pass a soft circular glow at her hand, most dissolve into fine sparkling mist. Only three or four come out the other side, sharper and brighter, lined up neatly on a tall glowing glass panel, each with a tiny soft check mark.
```

**Reads as:** lots of noise in, a few trusted answers out, one person in control. The safest option.

## Option 2: The guiding light (woman plus a buddy that is just light)

```
Scene: a young woman in a soft cream dress walks calmly through a vast, softly lit space filled with floating translucent cards (documents, charts, snippets) in drifting pastel clouds. Beside her, at shoulder height, floats a small warm glowing orb of pale gold and sky blue light, like a lantern with no handle. Its light falls in a gentle cone onto only a few cards ahead of her, making those few crisp and bright while the rest stay faded in the haze. She is looking where the light points. The orb is simple light, no face, no eyes, no body.
```

**Reads as:** a research buddy beside you that shows you where to look. It's the "buddy" idea without giving the AI a person's shape.

## Option 3: Two people, one screen (the buddy is a colleague, the AI is the glass)

```
Scene: two people stand side by side at a tall glowing glass panel, seen from the side and slightly behind: a young woman in a soft cream top and a man in a light shirt. She points at the panel while he leans in to look. On the panel, a few clean bright cards are neatly organized, each with a tiny soft check mark. Behind them, a large messy swirl of faded floating cards and misty pastel ribbons drifts away into the haze, as if left behind.
```

**Reads as:** people working together, with the AI as the tool on the glass. Two people also tend to feel warmer and more human in the feed.

## Option 4: How deep to go (woman at three soft levels)

```
Scene: a young woman in a soft cream dress stands at the top of three wide, shallow terraces of glowing pastel mist, stepping gently down into the distance like layers of a calm pool: the first shallow and pale, the second deeper lavender, the third deepest soft blue. Floating translucent cards (documents, charts, snippets) hover above each level, sparse on the top one and denser further down. A soft path of light runs from her feet down the terraces. She is pausing at the first step, calmly deciding how far to go.
```

**Reads as:** choosing how deep a question deserves, which is the skill's Scan, Standard and Deep levels. More abstract, and the most distinctive of the four.

---

## If a result misses

- **Too busy:** "Fewer cards, more empty cream space, softer haze."
- **The orb or glow looks like a character:** "Make the light a plain soft glow, no face and no shape."
- **Text or numbers on the cards:** "Remove all text and numbers, keep the cards abstract."
- **Figure too big:** "Make the person smaller, like a small figure in a large scene."

## Alt text (edit to match the option you pick)

> Soft pastel illustration of a woman in a bright, hazy space full of floating research cards. Most cards fade into mist, and only a few bright, checked ones stay clear.
